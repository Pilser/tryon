Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SaaSTryon ("the Service"), operated by Latiforge ("we", "us"), collects and uses information.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email, WhatsApp contact, and shop name. When you use the try-on feature we process the photos you upload (person and garment images) to generate previews.

2. How we use information

We use your information to provide and improve the Service, authenticate you, and communicate about your account. Uploaded photos are processed by our AI provider (Genlook) to generate try-on previews and are not used for other purposes.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the Service (hosting on Cloudflare, AI processing via Genlook).

4. Data retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting support@latiforge.com.

5. Cookies

We use a single session cookie (tryon_session) to keep you signed in across the platform and your shops. It is HttpOnly and Secure.

6. Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data by contacting support@latiforge.com.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@latiforge.com.
